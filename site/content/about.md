Learn Vulgultra is a free site to learn Vulgultra, a constructed language that runs the Vulgar Latin process in reverse and takes each word in the shortest form the Romance languages attest.

This site is only for learning the language. The grammar, the word lists and the evidence behind them are in the [project's repository](https://github.com/juleshenry/vulgultra).

## Under Construction

The lessons are not written yet. Every lesson page is in place and says so until its text arrives.
