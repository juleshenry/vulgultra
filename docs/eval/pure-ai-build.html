Verb system, built by rule

The counterpart to the hand picks in verb_picks.html. Nothing here is picked by hand, and no usage counts are used. Rebuild with .venv/bin/python3 scripts/build_pure_ai_verbs.py.

The rules

  1. Only the daughters' own forms. Every cell is a form some daughter has for that very cell: for the four regular themes, an ending after the stem that the lect uses on at least 5 verbs of the class; for esse, stare and habere, the whole word. Nothing is cut and glued: no form is new. Letters are compared without accents.
  2. Legal. Every form passes the spec's syllable rules; a regular ending is checked on a stem ending in one consonant and on one ending in two (kant-, lav-; tem-, respond-; …).
  3. Base plus variation. Six person endings serve every tense and every verb. Each tense of each verb has one base: a theme marker, or an irregular verb's stem. A cell that is not its base + its person ending is the verb's own form, a variation asset. A person ending must be one its lect uses for that person in at least two tenses, and never a false friend: an ending the daughters give the other persons, together, at least twice as often.
  4. Unique, with minor overloading. The persons of a tense differ. Two cells may be alike where more than half of the daughters' rows that have both have them alike: cond. 1sg = cond. 3sg (40 of 65); imp. 1pl = pres. 1pl (5 of 8); imp. 1pl = subj. pres. 1pl (8 of 8); imp. 2pl = pres. 2pl (5 of 8); imp. 2pl = subj. pres. 2pl (5 of 8); imp. 2sg = pres. 3sg (7 of 8); imp. 3pl = subj. pres. 3pl (7 of 8); imp. 3sg = subj. pres. 1sg (7 of 8); imp. 3sg = subj. pres. 3sg (7 of 8); plup. 1sg = plup. 3sg (12 of 17); subj. fut. 1sg = subj. fut. 3sg (12 of 12); subj. impf. 1sg = subj. impf. 3sg (45 of 58); subj. pres. 1sg = subj. pres. 3sg (52 of 59). A subjunctive 1sg is never alike with a form of another tense, but where the daughters allow it. Beyond that, at most 2 pairs of forms in a paradigm may be alike. Esse, stare and habere never share a form.
  5. Minimal. Of what passes, the system whose distinct pieces have the fewest letters: the six person endings, each base once, and each own form whole plus one. Then the fewest letters over all the forms; then the forms more daughters have.

Result

  • 538 letters of pieces: proven optimal (CP-SAT, 117 s).
  • 320 of the 402 cells are base + person ending; 82 are own forms.
  • 4.32 letters per form on average (after the stem, for the regular themes).
  • ✓ every form is a daughter's own form for that cell
  • ✓ every form legal (regular endings on both test stems)
  • ✓ six different person endings, none a false friend
  • ✓ every cell not marked as an own form is base + person ending
  • ✓ the persons of every tense differ, but for 1sg and 3sg where the daughters allow
  • ✓ no subjunctive 1sg alike with a form of another tense, but where the daughters allow
  • ✓ at most 2 pairs alike in any paradigm beyond the daughters' own
  • ✓ esse, stare and habere never alike
