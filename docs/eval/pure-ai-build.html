Verb system, built by rule

The counterpart to the hand picks in verb_picks.html. Nothing here is picked by hand, and no usage counts are used. Rebuild with .venv/bin/python3 scripts/build_pure_ai_verbs.py.

The rules

  1. Built from pieces. Every form is stem + marker (one per theme and tense) + person ending. Pieces are written together. Two vowels that meet stay, read as the spec reads them: i or u beside a vowel is a glide (io /jo/, ai /aj/), one syllable; only identical vowels merge (a-a → a).
  2. Attested only. Every piece is cut from a daughter's endings. A marker is what at least four of a row's six endings share at the front; a person ending is what follows it, and counts only if its lect uses it for that person in two tenses or more; an irregular stem is the verb's form minus that lect's own ending, found at least twice.
  3. Legal, and no false friends. Every form passes the spec's syllable rules, checked for each theme on a stem ending in one consonant and on one ending in two (tem- and respond-), so no piece leaves a cluster bare. A person never takes an ending the daughters give the other persons, together, at least twice as often: -s is the 2sg in most lects, so it cannot be the 1sg. Counted by lect and person: a lect with -s for two persons counts twice.
  4. Unique, with minor overloading. The persons of a tense always differ, except that 1sg and 3sg may agree (cantaba, cante), which is not counted as overloading; a subjunctive 1sg is never alike with a form of another tense; otherwise at most 2 pairs of forms in a paradigm may be alike; esse, stare and habere never share a form.
  5. Base plus variation. One set of person endings serves every tense and every theme. A theme may borrow another theme's marker for a tense (as Spanish folded -ere into -er), and must where its own rows give none or none that works; an irregular verb may take a second stem. Each such variation costs as much as 20 letters, so it is taken only where it makes a tense possible or saves more than about three letters on each of its forms.
  6. Minimal. Of what passes, the forms with the fewest letters after the stem, every cell counted once, plus the cost of the variations. Ties go to the pieces more daughters use.

Result

  • 2.85 letters after the stem per form, on average (1,177 letters over 413 forms).
  • Variation assets: 3: borrowed markers for the re theme's imp., the re theme's pres., the re theme's subj. fut.; 0 extra stems for the irregular verbs.
  • 101 of the 236 regular cells, and 8 of the 177 irregular ones, are a daughter's own form whole; the rest (marked new) are new combinations of attested pieces, each shown with the lects it comes from.
  • With these person endings and no pairs alike at all, the forms would average 2.85 letters after the stem.
  • Person endings found by local search from 8 fixed starting sets (3,135 sets weighed, each with its best markers found exactly). 5 of them end on this base; the others stop on costlier ones. No change of two persons' endings at once improves on it either. Nothing random is involved: the same result on every run.
  • ✓ every piece attested in a daughter
  • ✓ every form legal, on both test stems
  • ✓ the persons of every tense distinct, but for 1sg and 3sg
  • ✓ no subjunctive 1sg alike with a form of another tense
  • ✓ at most 2 pairs alike in any paradigm
  • ✓ esse, stare and habere never alike
