Verb system, built by rule

The counterpart to the hand picks in verb_picks.html. Nothing here is picked by hand, and no usage counts are used. Rebuild with .venv/bin/python3 scripts/build_pure_ai_verbs.py.

The rules

  1. Only the daughters' own forms. Every cell is a form some daughter has for that very cell: for the four regular themes, an ending after the stem that the lect uses on at least 5 verbs of the class; for esse, stare and habere, the whole word. No form is new. A lect's table that repeats another of its tenses ending for ending is taken to be filed under the wrong tense as well: it is kept under the tense whose other daughters share more of its endings and dropped from the other, or kept in both where they share as many (dropped: gsc -ir cond. (kept as impf.); lmo -à subj. pres. (kept as pres.); oc -re pret. (kept as fut.); ruq I-ez subj. pres. (kept as pres.); scn -ari subj. pres. (kept as pres.); scn -iri pres. (kept as subj. pres.)).
  2. In phonemes. Every form is read with its lect's own pronunciation and turned into Vulgultra phonemes, as the roots are. A regular ending is read on the stem tip-, which every lect spells and reads alike, and the stem's phonemes are taken off: French tipent is /tip/, so its ending is no phoneme at all. Forms are compared, counted and checked as phonemes.
  3. Legal. Every form passes the spec's syllable rules; a regular ending is checked on a stem ending in one consonant and on one ending in two (kant-, lav-; tem-, respond-; …).
  4. Each tense is one daughter's row. The six persons of a tense are taken together from one lect, as that lect has them (lects with the same row in phonemes share it), so a tense never mixes daughters. Lects whose endings the harvest cut after each tense's own stem are left out (an estar, an haber, an haber-ie, an ir, an ser, ca anar, ca estar, ca haver, ca ser, co -e, dlm avar, dlm saite, dlm zer, eml -ir, eml unknown, es estar, es haber, es ir, es ser, ext estar, ext haber, ext ir, ext ser, ext venir, fr aller, fr avoir, fr other, fr être, frp -ir, frp -re, frp other, gallo aler, gallo avair, gallo étr, gallo ói, gl estar, gl haber, gl ir, gl other, gl ser, gsc -ar, gsc -er, gsc -ir, gsc -re, ist avì, ist ièsi, it andare, it avere, it dare, it essere, it fare, it other, it stare, lij -ê, lmo lmo-conj-avè, nrf nrf-conj-avaer, nrf nrf-conj-aver, nrf unknown, pcd -te, pcd aller, pcd avoér, pcd dvoér, pcd foaire, pcd other, pcd s'in aler, pcd ête, pms -è, pms other, pt estar, pt haver, pt ir, pt other, pt ser, pt ter, rgn -êr, rgn rgn-conj-avér, rgn rgn-conj-vlér, ro avea, ro fi, ro vrea, ruo I-å, ruo II-é, ruo III-e, ruo IV-éi, ruo IV-í, rup unknown, ruq I, ruq I-ez, ruq II, ruq III, ruq IV-esc, ruq fi, ruq other, vec unknown, wa -eur, wa wa-conj-aveur).
  5. Base plus variation. Six person endings serve every tense and every verb. Each tense of each verb has one base, which at least half the persons of its row are built on: a theme marker (possibly none), or an irregular verb's stem (never none). A form that is not its base + its person ending is a variation asset: an own ending on the base, or an own form off it. A person ending must be one its lect uses for that person in at least two tenses, and never a false friend: an ending the daughters give the other persons, together, at least twice as often.
  6. Unique, with minor overloading. The persons of a tense differ. Two cells may be alike where more than half of the theme's or verb's own rows in the daughters that have both have them alike (listed with each paradigm). Beyond that, at most 2 pairs of forms in a paradigm may be alike, or, where no choice of the daughters' rows gets a paradigm down to that, the fewest it can manage. Esse, stare and habere never share a form.
  7. Minimal. Of what passes, the system whose distinct pieces have the fewest phonemes: the six person endings, each base once, and each own ending or own form, plus one for each of those. Then the fewest phonemes over all the forms; then the forms more daughters have.

Result

  • 571 phonemes of pieces: proven optimal (CP-SAT, 235 s).
  • Of the 402 cells, 287 are base + person ending, 68 have an own ending, and 47 are own forms.
  • 4.44 phonemes per form on average (after the stem, for the regular themes).
  • ✓ every form is a daughter's own form for that cell
  • ✓ every form legal (regular endings on both test stems)
  • ✓ six different person endings, none a false friend
  • ✓ every tense is one daughter's own row
  • ✓ every form marked regular is base + person ending
  • ✓ the persons of every tense differ, but for 1sg and 3sg where the daughters allow
  • ✓ at most 2 pairs alike in any paradigm beyond the daughters' own, or the fewest it can manage
  • ✓ esse, stare and habere never alike
