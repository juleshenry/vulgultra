\chapter{Scope and Design Philosophy}
\label{ch:scope}

\section{What Vulgultra Is}

The name is Latin on both sides: \emph{vulgus} (the common speech that
became Vulgar Latin) + \emph{ultra} (beyond). Not a Greek \emph{cyber-}
clipping.

Vulgultra is the reverse of the Vulgar Latin process, with the result packed
into as few phonemic syllables as the attested Romance \emph{daughter}
record allows.

Vulgar Latin \emph{lost} case, merged vowels, dropped final consonants,
turned $sC$-onsets into $e$-prothesis in the West, and (in French)
shifted stress onto the last remaining syllable. Vulgultra \emph{undoes}
those losses where a short attested daughter form still exists, and
otherwise keeps the short form. It is not a pidgin and not a
relexification. Latin is the process being reversed, not a competing
lect.

The packing problem begins with a meaning-aligned grid: each concept/sense
is a row and each daughter lect is a column. A cell may contain several
attested forms, or remain unknown. Stage one keeps the shortest legal
syllable count per row. Simulated annealing then chooses only among those
ties, maximizing the union of IPA segments in the selected roots.

\section{Axioms (priority order)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Minimize syllable count.} Phonemic $\sigma$, not letters.
        This outranks every other preference.
  \item \textbf{Daughters only.} Source lects $L$ are Romance daughters,
        grouped by branch (Ibero, Occitano, Oil, Arpitan, Gallo-Italian,
        Italo-Dalmatian, Rhaeto, Sardinian, Eastern). Latin and English
        are reserved tables, never $x_{c,\ell}$.
        $N_{\mathrm{src}}=|L|$ (currently 36).
  \item \textbf{Attested only.} Never invent a stem by clipping
        (\texttt{gat} is Catalan, not Spanish \texttt{gato} minus
        \texttt{-o}). Never pad a thin lect from a sister.
        Repair may alter a candidate; it may not mint a
        new lexeme. Empty cells are legal: that lect simply does not
        compete for that concept.
  \item \textbf{Maximize root contrast on the $\sigma$-slice.}
        There is no prescribed inventory size. Every selected root segment must
        occur in a candidate form from the active grid. Missing data is not
        a lexical gap. Source spread and morpheme uniformity are not scoring
        criteria. Endings are selected separately after roots, so productive
        endings cannot pre-fill the root inventory.
  \item \textbf{One-syllable nominal endings.} Every noun and adjective
        case cell is $1\sigma$; a $2\sigma$ nominal form
        ($-\bar{o}rum$) is clipped to its last syllable. Verb cells are
        \emph{not} clipped: an attested person ending is kept whole
        (\texttt{amos} stays \texttt{amos}). Axiom~1 still prefers the
        candidate with fewer syllables for each verb cell.
  \item \textbf{Restore case; keep gender.} Reverse VL. Nouns and
        adjectives inflect gender $\times$ case $\times$ number.
        Inherited Latin verb syncretism is allowed, across tenses and
        inside a tense row.
  \item \textbf{Phonotactic legality after repair.} Repair first
        (glide formation, identical-vowel collapse, last-resort
        epenthesis). A \emph{glide} is an $i/u$ pronounced as a
        consonant ($j$, $w$) that makes no syllable; a \emph{diphthong}
        is a vowel plus a glide in one syllable; a \emph{hiatus} is two
        adjacent vowels in separate syllables (es \texttt{co-mí-a}).
        Glide formation covers both diphthongs: $i/u$
        before a vowel ($ua \to wa$) and after one ($ai \to aj$,
        $eu \to ew$), so \texttt{-áis}, \texttt{-ai}, \texttt{-eu}
        are $1\sigma$. Unrepairable candidates are discarded. Do not
        use $w_{\mathrm{tact}}=500{,}000$ as a donor-glitch death
        sentence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The knapsack, and whether a global minimum exists}

Write $x_{c,\ell}\in\{0,1\}$ for ``concept $c$ takes the repaired
candidate from lect $\ell$''.

\begin{equation}
\min \sum_{c}\sum_{\ell} \sigma(c,\ell)\, x_{c,\ell}
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\sum_{\ell} x_{c,\ell}=1,
\quad x_{c,\ell}=0 \text{ if } c,\ell \text{ is unrepairable.}
\end{equation}

The $\sigma$-layer \textbf{still has a unique minimum}: independently
take a legal shortest candidate per concept. Among those $\sigma$-optimal
slices, maximize the union of observed segments:

\[
\min\sigma \;\prec\; \max|\Phi_{root}|.
\]

The first stage enforces $\min\sigma$ as a hard shortlist. Inside that
slice, the only lexical tie-break is $-|\Phi_{root}|$; each newly observed segment
lowers the energy by one. There is no numeric phoneme ceiling, lect-coverage
bonus, or morpheme-uniformity bonus.

Greedy initialization visits constrained concepts first and prefers a
candidate that adds the most new segments. Simulated annealing then explores
the same $\sigma$-optimal slice.

Endings stay coupled: one $1\sigma$ nominal table with distinct cells,
and one verb table whose cells keep their attested length.

\chapter{Phonology}
\label{ch:phonology}

\section{Inventory}

The root segment inventory $\Phi_{root}$ is emergent: it is the union of the
IPA segments in selected, grid-attested roots. No closed phoneme list or
numeric ceiling is imposed. PanPhon segments the transcriptions and supplies
features used to distinguish vowel nuclei, consonants, sonorants, and
obstruents for syllabification and phonotactic checks; those feature classes
are not a prescribed Vulgultra inventory. A conventional spelling map is
used where available, while other segments are shown in explicit IPA brackets.

Geminates are legal (including Italo-Romance and Sardinian forms). Glides
remain non-syllabic. Inflection may use either attested lect tables or a
productive \texttt{grid-inventory} block made from legal $V$, $CV$, $VC$,
and $CVC$ forms over segments observed in the shortlisted root grid. These
combinations are proposals, not claimed attested morphemes, and do not cap
the root inventory.

\section{Syllable template}

\[
\sigma = (s)(C_1)(C_2) V (C_3)(C_4)
\]

Legal onsets (reverse of Western $e$-prothesis; Latin $sC$ was an onset):
\begin{itemize}
  \item any single $C$
  \item obstruent + liquid/glide; sonorant + glide ($nj$, $lw$)
  \item $s/\textipa{S}/z + C$; $s/\textipa{S} + C +$ liquid ($stra$)
\end{itemize}

Legal codas: any single $C$; $CC$ if sonorant$+C$ or $s/\textipa{S}+$stop
(Latin \emph{est}, \emph{port}). Geminates are heterosyllabic
($ak.kwa$) and do not add a syllable.

Syllable \emph{count} is the number of vowels (minimum 1). One
algorithm for every daughter. Thin lects do not get a private
syllabifier; they get better IPA. Donor G2P is a last resort.

\section{Repair (before scoring)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item $iV \to jV$, $uV \to wV$ (acqua $/akkua/ \to /akkwa/$, $2\sigma$).
  \item Collapse identical vowels (Romanian \emph{cîine} /tS-iine/ $\to$ /tS-ine/).
  \item Last resort: epenthetic $/e/$ in an illegal cluster (adds $\sigma$).
  \item Still illegal $\to$ discard. Never keep it with a $500{,}000$ fine.
\end{enumerate}

Segment merges are not repair. They are listed, each with its reason, in
section~\ref{sec:merges}.

\section{Merges}
\label{sec:merges}

\emph{Approved 5 October 2026 and applied at transcription. Examples are
from the 213-concept grid.}

The inventory is emergent, so a merge is always a decision. Every merge is
written here with its reason, and code merges nothing this section does not
list. A merge needs one of two grounds.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Not a contrast.} In the lects named, the segment is a
        predictable variant of another sound. It is in the grid only
        because their transcription is narrower than the others'. The
        merge applies in those lects alone, so no daughter loses a
        distinction.
  \item \textbf{Reverse Vulgar Latin.} The segment arose in Vulgar Latin
        from a sequence, and Vulgultra restores the sequence. The
        distinction survives as a cluster.
\end{enumerate}

A segment that some daughter uses to tell two words apart is kept,
however rare it is. ``Another lect pronounces the same category
differently'' is not a ground: French \textipa{\;R} and Italian $r$ are
different sounds, and both stay.

\subsection{Kept}

\textbf{All four rhotics.} Spanish, Catalan, Portuguese and Galician
contrast a single \textipa{R} with a strong $r$ (\emph{caro} /
\emph{carro}); Portuguese pronounces the strong one \textipa{[K]}; the
O\"il lects have one rhotic, \textipa{\;R}. The Italian-type
transcriptions write $r$ for both. None is merged into another.

Each of these also separates words in at least one daughter and is kept:
the open mid vowels \textipa{E O}; the front rounded vowels
$y$ \textipa{\o} \textipa{\oe} and their glide \textipa{4}; the central
vowels \textipa{@ 1} and Ladin \textipa{5}; Istro-Romanian \textipa{6}
and \textipa{\ae}; French \textipa{A}; the nasal vowels; \textipa{Z},
\textipa{dZ}, $ts$, $dz$, $h$, \textipa{T}, Romagnol \textipa{D} and
Spanish /x/; and \textipa{N}, which is a variant of /n/ in Catalan and
Occitan but a phoneme in Ligurian and Emilian.

\section{Stress}

\textbf{Penultimate} by default, unmarked. A word whose source form is
stressed elsewhere keeps the source lect's stress, and an accent on the
stressed vowel marks it.

The default is calculated, not aesthetic: Classical Latin was (mostly)
penultimate; VL loss of the final syllable produced French oxytones;
reverse VL restores a $1\sigma$ nominal ending, so stress lands on the
last syllable of the stem $=$ penultimate of the word (a $2\sigma$ verb
ending such as \texttt{amos} carries the penult itself). Consensus of the
family that still has a final vowel.

\emph{Not in code yet: transcription drops the source's stress, so no
word carries an accent. Which accent mark is used is settled with the
spelling of the kept vowels, since the open vowels may take one too.}

\chapter{Morphology}
\label{ch:morphology}

Word $=$ (prefix) $+$ root $+$ ending: $1\sigma$ for nouns and
adjectives, attested length for verbs.

\section{Nouns: gender $\times$ case $\times$ number}

Case is restored (nom / acc / gen). Gender is kept. Number is kept.
That is $2\times 3\times 2 = 12$ cells, all $1\sigma$:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
M / F & NOM & ACC & GEN \\
\midrule
SG & $-o$ / $-a$ & $-on$ / $-an$ & $-is$ / $-es$ \\
PL & $-i$ / $-e$  & $-os$ / $-as$ & $-or$ / $-ar$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Acc.sg restores Latin $-m$ as $-n$. Gen.sg $-is/-es$ undoes the VL
merger that made gen.sg $=$ nom.pl. Acc.pl keeps the $-s$ plural.
Gen.pl clips $-\bar{o}rum / -\bar{a}rum$ to $1\sigma$.

Theme vowel $o/u/e$ is picked as a \emph{block} from the source-inventory
class (Ibero and Gallo-Italian $o$, Sardinian--Eastern $u$, Occitano and
Oil $e$). Corsican is $u$ with Sicilian; Megleno-Romanian is $u$ with
Romanian; Romagnol is $o$ with the other Gallo-Italian lects, not a
clone of Emilian. The case \emph{layer} is Latin, the theme is the
class. Verb person cells may mix lects (kept at attested length): that is
the one place 36 tables are real.

\section{Adjectives}

The same 12-cell table. Adjectives agree in gender, case, and number.

\section{Articles and copula}

Definite articles $o/a/os/as$ are closed-class definiteness markers
(Portuguese, one vowel). They do not carry case; the noun does.
Copula is suppletive present: $so, es, e, som, sos, son$.

\section{Verbs}

One lexical stem. Each finite cell (person $\times$ tense/mood) is
chosen on its own from any lect; a row may mix lects. An attested
ending is kept whole (\texttt{amos} is not clipped); among candidates
for a cell, fewer syllables win. $3\times 2\times 3\times 2=36$
finite cells plus 5 non-finite.

Among equal-length legal candidates for a cell, prefer
\emph{concordance}: a shared person coda with already-chosen cells of
the same person across earlier tenses/moods (present 1pl \texttt{ons}
tips a tied future toward \texttt{erons}). Concordance never spends an
extra syllable.

Finite verb cells carry no collision or distinctiveness constraint:
two persons may share an ending, inside a row or across tenses
(Venetian 3sg $=$ 3pl). The 5 non-finite cells stay mutually distinct.

Inchoative infix (Spanish \texttt{-zc-} \emph{conocer}, Italian
\texttt{-isc-} \emph{finire}, Romanian \texttt{-ez}/\texttt{-esc},
Catalan \texttt{-eix-}, Occitan/French \texttt{-iss-}) is a stem
allomorph, not part of the person ending. It is stripped into the stem
before ending extraction, and an inchoative verb joins its conjugation
class. Regular 1sg is \texttt{-o} (\emph{como}, \emph{escribo}).

\chapter{Energy}
\label{ch:energy}

During root annealing, only the first term varies:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
E_{root} &= -|\Phi_{root}| \\
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

After root selection, morphology uses:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
E_{morph} &= -|\Phi_{root}\cup\Phi_{ending}|
  + 200\sum_e \sigma(e)
  + 100000\cdot\mathrm{coll}
  + 2000\cdot\mathrm{viol}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Here $\mathrm{coll}$ counts identical pairs among the 12 noun cells and
among the 5 non-finite verb cells. Finite verb cells are not counted and
there is no distance term.

The shortest legal candidate slice is a hard input constraint, so syllables
cannot be traded against anything else. During root annealing, $\Phi_{root}$
is the union of segments in selected roots; each additional observed root
segment lowers energy by one. Endings are fixed while roots anneal. After
roots are chosen, table selection uses $-|\Phi_{root}\cup\Phi_{ending}|$ with
the same ending legality and collision terms, so a table is
rewarded for contributing new segments without making lexical choices moot.
There is no fixed cap. Lect and evidence remain available for inspection but
do not bias lexical selection.

Init: greedy maximum-contrast selection on the min-$\sigma$ slice. SA may
swap roots inside that slice only to improve the root segment union.

Endings: each finite cell is chosen on its own. Concordance (shared
person coda across tenses) breaks length ties. Noun theme stays one
block.

\chapter{Syntax}

SVO default. Case permits scrambling for focus. Adjectives follow the
noun and agree. Pro-drop. Complementizer particle $ke$. Negation: one
preverbal $1\sigma$ particle. Passive: periphrastic ($e$ $+$
participle), not 36 more fusional cells. Numerals: decimal, $1\sigma$
roots $0$--$10$ chosen like any other concept.

\chapter{Pipeline}

Extract $\to$ G2P $\to$ adapt $\to$ \textbf{repair} $\to$ discard
unrepairable $\to$ shortlist roots $\to$ enumerate endings $\to$ anneal
root ties $\to$ inflect.
Empty Swadesh cells are filled from per-lect \texttt{words.json} only on
an exact English gloss (no sister pad).

Sister G2P follows the branch (Spanish for Ibero foils, French for Oil
and Arpitan, Italian for Gallo-Italian / Italo-Dalmatian / Rhaeto,
Romanian for Eastern). That is a known bias. Prefer Kaikki/Wiktionary
IPA when it exists. Syllable count is always on the repaired Vulgultra
string, never on raw orthography.

\chapter{Closed against this spec}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Declension classes: a handful of theme blocks ($o/u/e$), not
        $|L|$ mixed case tables, not an unbounded search.
  \item Sources: Romance daughters only. Latin/English reserved.
  \item Articles: yes, definiteness only.
  \item Copula: yes, suppletive.
  \item Relatives: particle $ke$.
  \item Numerals: decimal.
  \item Passive: periphrastic.
  \item Case: kept (nom/acc/gen).
  \item Gender on nouns and adjectives: kept.
  \item Gemination: kept. Expand $\textipa{E}$ $\textipa{O}$ only if
        $1\sigma$ noun cells still collide.
\end{itemize}
