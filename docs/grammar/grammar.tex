\chapter{Scope and Design Philosophy}
\label{ch:scope}

\section{What Vulgultra Is}

The name is Latin on both sides: \emph{vulgus} (the common speech that
became Vulgar Latin) + \emph{ultra} (beyond). Not a Greek \emph{cyber-}
clipping.

Vulgultra is the reverse of the Vulgar Latin process, with the result packed
into as few phonemic syllables as the attested Romance \emph{daughter}
record allows.

Vulgar Latin \emph{lost} case, merged vowels, dropped final consonants,
turned $sC$-onsets into $e$-prothesis in the West, and (in French)
shifted stress onto the last remaining syllable. Vulgultra \emph{undoes}
those losses where a short attested daughter form still exists, and
otherwise keeps the short form. It is not a pidgin and not a
relexification. Latin is the process being reversed, not a competing
lect.

The packing problem is a knapsack. Each concept is an item. Each
daughter lect offers at most one candidate stem (after adaptation and
repair; an empty cell is not a candidate). You pick exactly one.
Primary cost is phonemic syllables. Inventory $\Phi$ and source
diversity couple the roots, so simulated annealing is the solver for
that coupled layer --- never for buying an extra syllable.

\section{Axioms (priority order)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Minimize syllable count.} Phonemic $\sigma$, not letters.
        This outranks every other preference.
  \item \textbf{Daughters only.} Source lects $L$ are Romance daughters,
        grouped by branch (Ibero, Occitano, Oil, Arpitan, Gallo-Italian,
        Italo-Dalmatian, Rhaeto, Sardinian, Eastern). Latin and English
        are reserved tables, never $x_{c,\ell}$.
        $N_{\mathrm{src}}=|L|$ (currently 36).
  \item \textbf{Attested only.} Never invent a stem by clipping
        (\texttt{gat} is Catalan, not Spanish \texttt{gato} minus
        \texttt{-o}). Never pad a thin lect from a sister.
        Repair may alter a candidate; it may not mint a
        new lexeme. Empty cells are legal: that lect simply does not
        compete for that concept.
  \item \textbf{Obscure lects only when they qualify.} A thin lect wins
        a root only on a $\sigma$-tie (never by adding a syllable) and
        only if it does not enlarge $\Phi$. On that remaining tie,
        maximize the number of distinct source lects.
  \item \textbf{One-syllable endings.} Every fusional cell is $1\sigma$.
        A $2\sigma$ person form (\texttt{amos}) is clipped to its last
        syllable (\texttt{mos}): that extra $\sigma$ is a theme already
        on the stem.
  \item \textbf{Restore case; keep gender.} Reverse VL. Nouns and
        adjectives inflect gender $\times$ case $\times$ number.
        Inherited Latin syncretism is allowed across tenses, not
        inside a tense row.
  \item \textbf{Phonotactic legality after repair.} Repair first
        (glide formation, identical-vowel collapse, last-resort
        epenthesis). Unrepairable candidates are discarded. Do not
        use $w_{\mathrm{tact}}=500{,}000$ as a donor-glitch death
        sentence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The knapsack, and whether a global minimum exists}

Write $x_{c,\ell}\in\{0,1\}$ for ``concept $c$ takes the repaired
candidate from lect $\ell$''.

\begin{equation}
\min \sum_{c}\sum_{\ell} \sigma(c,\ell)\, x_{c,\ell}
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\sum_{\ell} x_{c,\ell}=1,
\quad x_{c,\ell}=0 \text{ if } c,\ell \text{ is unrepairable.}
\end{equation}

The $\sigma$-layer \textbf{still has a unique minimum}: independently
take a legal shortest candidate per concept. Among those $\sigma$-optimal
slices, $\Phi$ and lect-diversity couple the choices:

\[
\min\sigma \;\prec\;
\min|\Phi| \;\prec\;
\max|\{\text{source lects}\}| \;\prec\;
\max\mathrm{mean\ support}.
\]

Weights encode that order so a lower term cannot buy a higher one.
$N_{\mathrm{src}}=|L|$ is not a frozen 34: adding a daughter must
still satisfy the middle inequality.

\[
23\,w_{\mathrm{phon}} < w_{\mathrm{syl}},
\qquad
|L|\,w_{\mathrm{div}} < w_{\mathrm{phon}},
\qquad
\overline{\mathrm{gap}}\,w_{\mathrm{norm}} < w_{\mathrm{div}}.
\]

With $w_{\mathrm{phon}}=40$ and $w_{\mathrm{div}}=1$, $|L|\le 39$.
Current $|L|=36$.

Greedy set-cover init (constrained concepts first, reuse $\Phi$, then
a new lect) is a heuristic for the coupled layers. SA on roots is
justified again --- only inside the $\sigma$-optimal slice.

Endings stay coupled (one $1\sigma$ table, distinct cells in a row).

\chapter{Phonology}
\label{ch:phonology}

\section{Inventory}

Ceiling: 18 consonants + 5 vowels $=23$. Under a fixed $\sigma$ total,
minimize $|\Phi|$ globally ($w_{\mathrm{phon}}=40$).

Consonants: $p b t d k g$, $m n$, $f v s z \textipa{S} \textipa{tS}$,
$l r$, $j w$. Geminates of these are legal (Italo-Romance, Sardinian).
Do \emph{not} ban gemination to force more vowels.

Vowels: $a e i o u$. If a $1\sigma$ ending row cannot be packed
without collision, \textbf{then} expand with $\textipa{E}$ $\textipa{O}$
(spelled \texttt{\`{e}} \texttt{\`{o}}). Reverse VL restored length and
quality last; gemination is the cheaper contrast.

Glides $j w$ are consonants. A GV or VG sequence is still one syllable
because there is one nucleus. They are not extra unit phonemes.

\section{Syllable template}

\[
\sigma = (s)(C_1)(C_2) V (C_3)(C_4)
\]

Legal onsets (reverse of Western $e$-prothesis; Latin $sC$ was an onset):
\begin{itemize}
  \item any single $C$
  \item obstruent + liquid/glide; sonorant + glide ($nj$, $lw$)
  \item $s/\textipa{S}/z + C$; $s/\textipa{S} + C +$ liquid ($stra$)
\end{itemize}

Legal codas: any single $C$; $CC$ if sonorant$+C$ or $s/\textipa{S}+$stop
(Latin \emph{est}, \emph{port}). Geminates are heterosyllabic
($ak.kwa$) and do not add a syllable.

Syllable \emph{count} is the number of vowels (minimum 1). One
algorithm for every daughter. Thin lects do not get a private
syllabifier; they get better IPA. Donor G2P is a last resort.

\section{Repair (before scoring)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item $iV \to jV$, $uV \to wV$ (acqua $/akkua/ \to /akkwa/$, $2\sigma$).
  \item Collapse identical vowels (c\^{i}ine $/t͡ʃiine/ \to /t͡ʃine/$).
  \item Last resort: epenthetic $/e/$ in an illegal cluster (adds $\sigma$).
  \item Still illegal $\to$ discard. Never keep it with a $500{,}000$ fine.
\end{enumerate}

Map $/ʝ/ \to /j/$, Romanian $/x/ \to /k/$ (not $/ks/$),
$/ɲ ʎ/ \to nj, lj$ (now legal onsets).

\section{Stress}

\textbf{Penultimate}, no exceptions, unmarked. Calculated, not
aesthetic: Classical Latin was (mostly) penultimate; VL loss of the
final syllable produced French oxytones; reverse VL restores a
$1\sigma$ ending, so stress lands on the last syllable of the stem
$=$ penultimate of the word. Consensus of the family that still has
a final vowel.

\chapter{Morphology}
\label{ch:morphology}

Word $=$ (prefix) $+$ root $+$ $1\sigma$ ending.

\section{Nouns: gender $\times$ case $\times$ number}

Case is restored (nom / acc / gen). Gender is kept. Number is kept.
That is $2\times 3\times 2 = 12$ cells, all $1\sigma$:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
M / F & NOM & ACC & GEN \\
\midrule
SG & $-o$ / $-a$ & $-on$ / $-an$ & $-is$ / $-es$ \\
PL & $-i$ / $-e$  & $-os$ / $-as$ & $-or$ / $-ar$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Acc.sg restores Latin $-m$ as $-n$. Gen.sg $-is/-es$ undoes the VL
merger that made gen.sg $=$ nom.pl. Acc.pl keeps the $-s$ plural.
Gen.pl clips $-$\={o}rum / $-$\={a}rum$ to $1\sigma$.

Theme vowel $o/u/e$ is picked as a \emph{block} from the source-inventory
class (Ibero and Gallo-Italian $o$, Sardinian--Eastern $u$, Occitano and
Oil $e$). Corsican is $u$ with Sicilian; Megleno-Romanian is $u$ with
Romanian; Romagnol is $o$ with the other Gallo-Italian lects, not a
clone of Emilian. The case \emph{layer} is Latin, the theme is the
class. Person rows stay per-lect (clipped to $1\sigma$): that is the
one place 36 tables are real.

\section{Adjectives}

The same 12-cell table. Adjectives agree in gender, case, and number.

\section{Articles and copula}

Definite articles $o/a/os/as$ are closed-class definiteness markers
(Portuguese, one vowel). They do not carry case; the noun does.
Copula is suppletive present: $so, es, e, som, sos, son$.

\section{Verbs}

One lexical stem. Each finite tense is its own 6-person row, taken
from one lect, and tenses may come from different lects. The tense
theme and the person endings of that row travel together
(\texttt{amos} clips to \texttt{mos}). $3\times 2\times 3\times 2=36$
finite cells plus 5 non-finite. Every cell is $1\sigma$.

Collision and $d\ge 2$ distinctiveness apply \textbf{inside a 6-person
row}, not across tenses. Romance syncretism across tenses is legal;
that is what makes $1\sigma$ packing possible. Zero collision across
all 41 cells is un-Romance and forces $2\sigma$ endings.

\chapter{Energy}
\label{ch:energy}

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
E &= 1000\sum_r \sigma(r)
  + 40\,|\Phi|
  + 1\cdot(N_{\mathrm{src}}-|\{\ell_r\}|)
  + 0.02\cdot\overline{\mathrm{gap}} \\
  &\quad + 200\sum_e \sigma(e)
  + 100000\cdot\mathrm{coll}_{\mathrm{row}}
  + 2000\cdot\mathrm{viol}
  + 500\sum_{\mathrm{row}}\max(0,2-d)
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

$N_{\mathrm{src}}=|L|=36$. $|\{\ell_r\}|$ is how many distinct lects own a
selected root. $\overline{\mathrm{gap}}$ is mean $N_{\mathrm{src}}-\mathrm{support}$.
One extra syllable ($1000$) beats emptying $\Phi$ ($40\times 23=920$).
One extra phoneme ($40$) beats using every lect ($1\times 36=36$).
Diversity never lengthens a word and never adds a phoneme.

Init: set-cover on the min-$\sigma$ slice. SA may swap roots inside
that slice to shrink $\Phi$ or pick up an unused lect.

Endings: each tense row is chosen on its own. The six cells of a row
stay one lect. Noun theme stays one block.

\chapter{Syntax}

SVO default. Case permits scrambling for focus. Adjectives follow the
noun and agree. Pro-drop. Complementizer particle $ke$. Negation: one
preverbal $1\sigma$ particle. Passive: periphrastic ($e$ $+$
participle), not 36 more fusional cells. Numerals: decimal, $1\sigma$
roots $0$--$10$ chosen like any other concept.

\chapter{Pipeline}

Extract $\to$ G2P $\to$ adapt $\to$ \textbf{repair} $\to$ discard
unrepairable $\to$ knapsack roots $\to$ anneal endings $\to$ inflect.
Empty Swadesh cells are filled from per-lect \texttt{words.json} only on
an exact English gloss (no sister pad).

Sister G2P follows the branch (Spanish for Ibero foils, French for Oil
and Arpitan, Italian for Gallo-Italian / Italo-Dalmatian / Rhaeto,
Romanian for Eastern). That is a known bias. Prefer Kaikki/Wiktionary
IPA when it exists. Syllable count is always on the repaired Vulgultra
string, never on raw orthography.

\chapter{Closed against this spec}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Declension classes: a handful of theme blocks ($o/u/e$), not
        $|L|$ mixed case tables, not an unbounded search.
  \item Sources: Romance daughters only. Latin/English reserved.
  \item Articles: yes, definiteness only.
  \item Copula: yes, suppletive.
  \item Relatives: particle $ke$.
  \item Numerals: decimal.
  \item Passive: periphrastic.
  \item Case: kept (nom/acc/gen).
  \item Gender on nouns and adjectives: kept.
  \item Gemination: kept. Expand $\textipa{E}$ $\textipa{O}$ only if
        $1\sigma$ rows still collide.
\end{itemize}
